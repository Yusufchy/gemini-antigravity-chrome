How to Use Antigravity v2.1
1. Quick Start & Instant Audit

Click the toolbar icon or press Alt+Shift+A to open this panel. Click the top Pulse icon for an instant 100ms Core Web Vitals, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, and SEO audit.

2. Multi-Tab & PDF Synthesis (@)

Type @ in the prompt bar to attach open tabs or PDF documents so Antigravity can compare and synthesize data across tabs simultaneously.

3. Pick Element & Region Snip

Use Crosshair (Alt+Shift+X) to attach any element's DOM selector, Playwright locator, and computed CSS, or Crop to capture a screen region.

4. Macro Recorder & Playwright Codegen

Open the Clock drawer and click Record Macro to record your own clicks, inputs, and selects—replay them anytime or export a ready-to-run Playwright .spec.ts test.

5. 34 Autonomous MCP Tools & Guardrails

Includes dropdown/ARIA combobox selection, HTTP-only cookie & storage inspection, dark-mode/network/geo emulation, vector PDF export, GIF recording, and Auto / Ask safety modes.
