# Privacy Policy — Gemini Antigravity in Chrome

**Effective Date**: November 23, 2026  
**Publisher**: [VionixAI](https://vionixai.tech/)

Gemini Antigravity in Chrome is designed with strict local-first privacy:

1. **No Data Collection**: This extension does not collect, store on remote servers, sell, or share any personal information, browsing history, credentials, cookies, downloaded files, or page contents.
2. **100% Local Processing**: Communication occurs exclusively on your own machine over `127.0.0.1:17380` and Chrome Native Messaging (`com.google.antigravity.chrome_bridge`). Downloaded files are tracked and moved strictly between your local Chrome Downloads directory and your chosen local project folder.
3. **Local Settings Only**: User preferences (autonomy mode, shortcuts, domain allow/block lists, agent tab groups, and recorded macros) are stored locally via `chrome.storage.local` and `chrome.storage.session`.
4. **Contact**: Questions may be sent via [https://vionixai.tech/contact/](https://vionixai.tech/contact/).
